There is a booth on our street, between the goods counter and the growers' room, where somebody sits with headphones on and a mug going cold. This is what comes out of it.

The Daily Roach is the collective's journal. Notes on the craft — living soil, slow cures, the patience the forest teaches. Notes on the culture — what fifteen years around this plant in Knysna actually looks like, as against what people assume it looks like. And notes on the law, because knowing where the line is drawn is part of taking the plant seriously.

Nothing here is for sale. That is not a disclaimer, it is the point — this is the part of the shop where nobody sells you anything, and you are welcome to stay as long as you like.

New pages when there is something worth saying. If you want to talk instead of read, the growers' room is one window over.
