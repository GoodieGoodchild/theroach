🪳 THE ROACH DAILY JOURNAL — Peace, That's It 003

📅 7 September 2026 | 🌙 22:52
📍 Knysna, Garden Route


🌙 Peace 

Peace.

That's the whole thought tonight.

Not happiness. Not success. Not figuring everything out.

Just peace.

A quiet room. ☕
No drama.
No phone buzzing.
No problem demanding attention.

Just a moment where everything is okay.

Living around Knysna, I've realised peace doesn't always look impressive. Sometimes it's the wind outside, a quiet road, the fridge humming and knowing the people you care about are alright.

That's enough.

We spend so much time chasing the next thing that we forget to enjoy the moment where nothing needs fixing.

Tonight, I'm choosing that moment. 🖤

🪳 Night Owl Takeaway 

Peace doesn't need a big announcement.

Sometimes it's simply nothing going wrong for a while. 🌙
