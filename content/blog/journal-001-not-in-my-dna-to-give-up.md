But fuck it all. 🤷‍♂️🔥

**I'm still here.**

Still breathing.\
Still standing.\
Still building.\
Still moving forward. 🦾

I can't change what happened.\
I can't undo the lies.\
I can't rewrite the past.

**But I can decide what happens next.** 👊

And giving up isn't fucking part of the plan. 🧬🔥

One day at a time.\
One battle at a time.\
One comeback at a time. 🚀
