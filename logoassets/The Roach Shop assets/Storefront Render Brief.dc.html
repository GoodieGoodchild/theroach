Storefront render brief

/shop hero pair
v1 · for AI generation

Two photographic storefront renders, shot as if they sit side by side on the same dark street. They are the only imagery on the page, so they must feel like one continuous scene split in two — same street, same night, same camera.

1 · The one rule that matters

Render the sign boards EMPTY — no shop name, no text, no neon lettering.

The names, the leaf glyph and the glow are drawn live in the web page on top of the photo (that is how the sign flickers on, breathes, and reacts to hover). Any lettering baked into the render will collide with it. Leave the fascia as a dark empty panel with a faint inner shadow — but DO keep the coloured spill light on the wall and pavement, as if the sign above were already lit.

2 · Shared scene specification

FramingStraight-on, eye level, no perspective tilt. Shopfront fills the frame edge to edge; ~12% headroom above the fascia, pavement visible at the bottom.
Lens / camera35mm equivalent, f/4, tripod height 1.5m. Architectural, no fisheye, verticals perfectly parallel.
Time / lightNight. No daylight, no sky. Black surroundings falling to pure black at the frame edges. Wet, faintly reflective dark concrete pavement.
PaletteNear-black (#050505) base; deep forest-green joinery. Left shop spills mint green (#7CFFB2), right shop spills rose pink (#FF8FB8). No other hues.
Aspect ratiosSupply each shop in 5:4 (desktop), 3:4 (marquee layout) and 4:5 — or one generous 1:1 master per shop that crops safely to all three.
Delivery2400px on the long edge minimum, PNG or high-quality JPG, no watermark, no people, no readable brand logos on any product.

3 · Prompt — left shop (accessories)

Photoreal night photograph of a small boutique smoke-accessory storefront, straight-on architectural view, 35mm, f/4. Matte black timber shopfront with a wide empty sign fascia above the window — the fascia panel is completely blank, no text, no lettering, no logo — lit from within by soft mint-green light spill onto the wall and pavement. Large plate-glass window revealing three warm-lit shelves of clear and coloured glass bongs, pipes and grinders, and a lower counter of rolling-paper boxes and tins, all generic and unbranded. One small potted plant to the side. Wet dark concrete pavement with soft green reflections. Background pure black, no sky, no people, no signage text anywhere. Cinematic, high detail, luxury retail, moody low-key lighting.

4 · Prompt — right shop (flowers)

Photoreal night photograph of a small florist storefront, straight-on architectural view, 35mm, f/4, identical camera and street as its neighbour. Deep forest-green painted timber shopfront with a wide empty sign fascia above the window — the fascia panel is completely blank, no text, no lettering, no logo — bathed in soft rose-pink light spill onto the wall and pavement. Large window showing dried flower bunches hanging upside down from a rail, stone and concrete vases of dried blooms on the sill, apothecary jars on shelves behind. Wet dark concrete pavement with soft pink reflections. Background pure black, no sky, no people, no signage text anywhere. Cinematic, high detail, romantic, moody low-key lighting.

5 · Accept / reject checklist

Accept
Blank fascia, coloured spill still present Verticals parallel, no lens distortion Edges fall to true black for seamless tiling Both shops share one horizon and pavement line Products unbranded and generic
Reject
Any text, neon words or garbled AI lettering Daylight, sky, street furniture or passers-by Warm orange sodium street lighting Third colour entering the palette Perspective angle differing between the two

6 · Where they land

Drop each finished render straight onto its placeholder in The Roach Shop mockup — the left and right image slots accept a drag-and-drop and keep the file. The live neon lettering, the flicker-on, the hover glow and the age gate are already built around them.
